abstract: 'Governments increasingly use digital portals, automation, and AI to support immigration services, but current evaluation methods remain incomplete. Algorithmic audits focus on model accuracy and fairness, while digital-government benchmarks often overlook whether applicants can access, understand, and contest decisions. This paper introduces I-BRIDGE (Immigration Benchmark for Rights, Inclusion, Digital Governance, and Efficiency), an access-adjusted benchmark for AI-assisted immigration decision systems. I-BRIDGE models immigration as a three-layer public-service pipeline: access and submission, adjudication, and post-decision governance. It combines adjudicative quality with end-to-end opportunity, access-adjusted fairness gap, digital burden, explanation coverage, contestability, and a composite Inclusive Immigration AI Governance Score (IIGS).
