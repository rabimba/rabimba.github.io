abstract: Chain-of-thought prompting significantly improves the reasoning capabilities
  of large language models (LLMs) but incurs high inference costs due to lengthy autoregressive
  decoding. While previous efficiency approaches reduce costs by discarding tokens
  or utilizing continuous latent representations, these lossy methods fundamentally
  sacrifice interpretability and auditability. In this work, we introduce a novel
  compression framework for intermediate reasoning. We apply Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE)
  to reasoning corpora to learn a compressed vocabulary of Private Use Area (PUA)
  tokens, each representing frequent semantic spans. After expanding the LLM's vocabulary,
  we fine-tune the model via strictly 1:1, our method is lossless. We evaluate our
  approach on Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-14B, and Qwen2.5-32B across four diverse benchmarks.
