My research sits at the intersection of **AI reasoning**, **decentralized systems**, and **software security** — currently focused on making LLM-based agents safe enough for high-stakes environments like finance and security operations.

<div class="arc-grid">

<div class="arc-card">
<div class="arc-kicker">2023 — Now</div>
<div class="arc-title">LLM Reasoning &amp; Agentic AI</div>
<p>How do we make LLM-based agents trustworthy? Multi-agent architectures for code and test generation (HPCAgentTester, Smartify, UniTranslator), hallucination and rationality analysis, benchmark integrity for LLM serving (vLLM on OpenXLA), and AI safety for security-critical workflows — including LogInject, our USENIX Security 26 study of prompt injection in SOC log analysis.</p>
<div class="arc-links"><a href="/tags/llms/">#LLMs</a> <a href="/tags/ai-agents/">#AI Agents</a> <a href="/tags/security/">#Security</a></div>
</div>

<div class="arc-card">
<div class="arc-kicker">2019 — Present</div>
<div class="arc-title">Blockchain &amp; Systems Security</div>
<p>Decentralized infrastructure that doesn't trust any single party: TEE coordination across heterogeneous hardware (DHTee), decentralized FaaS, smart contract vulnerability detection and repair across Solidity and Move (Smartify — ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper), privacy-preserving transactions and fair exchange, and on-chain AI inference.</p>
<div class="arc-links"><a href="/tags/blockchain/">#Blockchain</a> <a href="/tags/smart-contracts/">#Smart Contracts</a> <a href="/tags/security/">#Security</a></div>
</div>

<div class="arc-card">
<div class="arc-kicker">2014 — 2018</div>
<div class="arc-title">Web, VR &amp; Emerging Interfaces</div>
<p>Where it started: optimizing Web Virtual Reality (Rice University thesis), WebXR and A-Frame, decentralized social VR in the browser, and speech interfaces — work that became a decade of community teaching through Mozilla and Google Developer programs.</p>
<div class="arc-links"><a href="/tags/webxr/">#WebXR</a> <a href="/tags/webvr/">#WebVR</a></div>
</div>

<div class="arc-card">
<div class="arc-kicker">2025 — Present</div>
<div class="arc-title">Quantum-Enhanced ML</div>
<p>Quantum contrastive word embeddings with variational circuits for near-term quantum devices (QuCoWE) — exploring what NISQ-era hardware can realistically contribute to representation learning.</p>
<div class="arc-links"><a href="/tags/quantum-computing/">#Quantum Computing</a></div>
</div>
