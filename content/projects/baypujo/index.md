[Bay Area Pujo Parikrama](https://rabimba.github.io/baypujo/) is a comprehensive community guide and route planner for Durga Puja celebrations across the San Francisco Bay Area.

**Key Features:**
- **31 Pujas Across 6 Regions** — complete listings covering South Bay, East Bay, Tri-Valley, Central Valley, Peninsula, and Sacramento
- **Parikrama Route Planner** — interactive routing tool to plan pandal-hopping itineraries across multiple celebrations in a single day
- **Real-Time Ritual Tracking** — schedules, bhog availability, ticketing info, and next-ritual countdowns aligned with the panjika calendar
- **Bilingual Experience** — Bengali and English cultural context, including Mahalaya traditions and historical background
