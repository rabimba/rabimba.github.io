[Houston Pujo Parikrama](https://rabimba.github.io/houstonpujo/) is a community-driven portal and route planner mapping Durga Puja celebrations across Greater Houston, Texas.

**Key Features:**
- **Regional Coverage** — includes major Houston institutions including Houston Durga Bari Society (Biswa Bangla Sarad Samman winner), SDKKM Temple, Mantra Pearland, and Maitri in Houston
- **Parikroma Route Planner** — route planning across Energy Corridor, West Houston, Southwest Houston / Pearland, and surrounding areas
- **Full Festival Calendars** — schedules spanning Mahalaya, Bodhan, Shashthi–Dashami, and Bijoya Sammilani
- **Community-First Details** — free entry tags, bhog timings, cultural program headliners, and direct navigation links
