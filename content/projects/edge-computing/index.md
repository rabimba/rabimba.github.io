This research area investigates how to bring secure, verifiable computation to resource-constrained edge devices:

- **Trusted Inference at the Edge** &mdash; Combining smart contracts with trusted execution environments for verifiable edge inference (IEEE ICBC 2024).
- **Blockchain + TEE** &mdash; Lessons learned from applying TEEs in blockchain applications, with implications for future secure systems.
- **Supporting Heterogeneous Infrastructure** &mdash; Protecting critical infrastructure across diverse computing environments (AsiaCCS 2023).
- **AI-Based Personalized Interventions** &mdash; Deploying AI models for health applications on edge devices (SynBio 2025).

This work bridges the gap between the security guarantees of centralized systems and the autonomy of decentralized, edge-deployed applications.
