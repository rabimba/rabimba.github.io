During my Mozilla Research Fellowship and MS at Rice University, I worked on making virtual reality accessible through web browsers:

- **Optimizing VR on WebGL** &mdash; Research on improving parallelism and rendering performance for WebVR applications in Firefox (MS Thesis, Rice University).
- **Mozilla Research Fellowship** &mdash; Conducted in-depth research to optimize VR performance in the browser, contributing to Firefox's WebVR capabilities.
- **Google Developer Expert** &mdash; Recognized expertise in Web Technologies, contributing to the broader web platform community.

This work laid the foundation for my continued interest in bringing powerful computing capabilities to resource-constrained environments, whether that's a browser or an edge device.
